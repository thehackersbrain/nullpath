# Cyber Craft Labs

**Cyber Craft Labs** is a **security R&D lab** founded by **[[ly4k]]**
(thehackersbrain). It's the professional research vehicle behind the
offensive-security work captured in this wiki — AD attack research, AD CS /
PKI, shadow credentials, reverse engineering, and malware
development/analysis. The wiki is the lab's working knowledge base.

## What it does

- **Offensive security R&D** — AD / Kerberos / AD CS / PKI attack research
  (the core of this wiki), plus reverse engineering and malware
  development/analysis for detection + authorized red-team tooling.
- **The research home** for the techniques and tooling in this wiki —
  [[ad-cs-esc-attacks]], [[shadow-credentials]], [[pkinit-unpac-the-hash]],
  the relay/RBCD family, etc.
- **Authorized work** — pentest engagements, red-team operations, CTF/lab
  environments (HTB), and security research/education.

## Why it matters to this wiki

- It's the **organizational context** for the knowledge base — the "who is
  doing this research" answer, tying the [[ly4k]] persona, the
  [[berserkarch]] distro, and the AD/AD CS research together.
- The operating frame for everything in the repo (authorized, legitimate
  security work) is the lab's frame.
